Everyday. Maximum. Sun. Three products for three different scalp needs.

Like all mineral sunscreens, Matte Shield may leave some visible cast when first applied.

It was developed to minimize that drawback. A subtle tint helps the zinc oxide formula blend naturally into the skin, and when it is massaged in, the cast fades as the product is absorbed, leaving a natural matte finish instead of a greasy sunscreen shine.

As with any tinted mineral product, the final look varies with skin tone and the amount applied.

Each one was developed to do a different job. They aren’t three versions of the same product.

Matte Look™ our lightweight daily anti-shine cream, for scalp and face.

Shine Block™ our strongest dedicated anti-shine scalp product, for more intensive and longer-lasting control.

Matte Shield™ tinted mineral SPF 40 with a natural matte finish, for exposed scalp and face.

Yes. It was developed around the needs of exposed scalp skin, particularly bald heads, shaved heads and scalp micropigmentation (SMP).
